Technical Portfolio

Certified components, engineered for performance, safety, and long-term reliability.

Premium energy-saving gearless motors imported from Turkey. Delivers quiet rides, low heat, and minimal power consumption.

Electric Control Panel

VVVF drive systems offering precise speed control, soft start/stop profiles, and protection against power surges.

COP & Hall Buttons

Modern design COP panels with crystal displays, tactile micro-travel keys, and multiple language indicator options.

Cabin Doors & Rails

High-precision guide rails and automatic sliding doors with multi-beam infrared light curtain protection systems.

Small Machine Room & MRL Passenger Elevators

Machine-Room-Less designs minimize shaft space and structural cost. Ideal for modern apartments, offices, and medium commercial buildings.

Hospital & Bed Elevators

Specifically sized for stretchers and hospital beds, with extra smooth low-acceleration curves for patient safety and comfort.

Villa & Luxury Home Elevators

Cargo & Industrial Freight Elevators

Heavy-duty lift platforms designed for warehouses, factories, and loading docks with high load capacity and durable interiors.

Automatic Rescue Device (ARD)

Moves the elevator to the nearest landing and opens doors automatically during power failures.

Full-Height Light Curtain

Multiple infrared beams detect any obstacle in the door path, preventing closure on passengers.

Overload Sensor Protection

Prevents lift operation and triggers an alarm when the cabin load exceeds rated capacity.

Emergency Alarm & Intercom

Direct intercom line connecting passengers to building management for immediate assistance.

Thermal Overload Protection

Motor protection against overheating to extend machine life and prevent operational shutdowns.

Door Lock Monitoring

Ensures the elevator only moves when all doors are fully locked and sealed, preventing accidents.
