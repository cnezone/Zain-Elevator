Premium Components We Use

We never compromise on internal components. Each lift is built with the finest industry-tested parts for maximum safety, efficiency, and longevity.

Turkish Gearless Motor

High-efficiency imported gearless traction machines from Turkey. Requires no oil, runs silently, and delivers a smooth jerk-free ride with minimal energy consumption.

VVVF Micro-processor Panel

Variable Voltage Variable Frequency drive for precision floor leveling. Reduces current surges on startup, extends component life, and ensures smooth starts and stops.

Safety Governor System

Overspeed governor with safety gear — if the lift exceeds the set speed, the mechanical safety clamps lock onto the guide rails to stop the car immediately.

Auto Door Interlock

Electromagnetic door locks prevent the cabin from moving unless all landing and cabin doors are fully and properly closed — a primary safety requirement.

Battery ARD System

Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup allows the lift to move to the nearest floor and open doors automatically during a power failure.

Hall & Cabin Buttons

Premium stainless steel call buttons with LED indicators and braille markings for all floors. Digital floor indicator displays with directional arrows for passenger awareness.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between MR and MRL elevators?

MR (Machine Room) elevators have a dedicated room above the shaft for the motor. MRL (Machine Room Less) elevators house the motor inside the shaft itself, saving space and construction cost — ideal for homes and small buildings.

How long does elevator installation take?

Installation typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on the number of floors and type of elevator. For a simple 3-floor home elevator, 10–15 days is standard. Complex commercial projects may take longer.

Do you provide after-sale maintenance?

Yes! We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) with scheduled monthly visits, 24/7 emergency rescue, and genuine spare parts replacement. See our Services page for details.

Are your elevators suitable for load-shedding areas?

Absolutely. All our elevators come with the Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) battery backup system that safely brings the lift to the nearest floor and opens doors during power failure.

What is the minimum pit depth required?

Standard pit depth is 1,000mm (1 meter). For home elevators with low-pit requirements, we can engineer solutions with as little as 200–300mm pit depth using special buffers.

Can you modernize an existing old elevator?

Yes. We specialize in elevator modernization — replacing old relay panels with VVVF drives, upgrading motors, refurbishing cabin interiors, and bringing older lifts up to current safety standards.
