Professional Installation

Certified Installation & Commissioning

Our experienced engineers follow a structured, step-by-step installation process that ensures your elevator operates safely and optimally from day one. We handle everything — from guide rail erection and machine mounting to complete electrical wiring and final safety certification.

  • 1
    Civil Preparation

    Verification of shaft dimensions, pit depth, and machine room specifications before any work begins.

  • 2
    Mechanical Installation

    Guide rail, sill, landing door, and cabin installation by our specialized mechanical team.

  • 3
    Electrical & Control Wiring

    VVVF panel setup, wiring of all hall and car buttons, safety circuits, and ARD battery system.

  • 4
    Safety Testing & Sign-off

    Full load tests, speed measurements, and verification of all safety devices before handover.

Upgrade Your Old Lift

Lift Modernization & Renovation

Is your existing elevator outdated, consuming excessive electricity, or frequently breaking down? Our modernization service can retrofit your old system with the latest technology at a fraction of new installation cost — delivering up to 40% energy savings and dramatically improved ride comfort.

  • Panel Replacement (Relay → VVVF)

    Upgrade old relay-based panels to modern VVVF micro-processor drives for precision control.

  • Gearless Motor Upgrade

    Replace old geared traction machines with silent, efficient Turkish gearless motors.

  • Cabin & Door Renovation

    Modern stainless steel cabin panels, LED lighting, and automatic sliding doors.

  • Safety Devices Upgrade

    New overspeed governor, safety gear, oil buffers, door locks, and ARD battery backup.
